EMBER CITY

Six years inside.
One last night to settle everything.

An open-world crime game in a neon city of nine districts. Play the story from Homecoming to Ember Falls, or go off on your own: rob the jewellery counter at the mall, crack the vault at the top of Ember Tower, sell hot cars at the chop shop, and ride the Ferris wheel on the pier. Rosy, your golden retriever, comes everywhere with you.

WHAT'S IN IT

  • Story modeFully staged cutscenes, player choices and two endings. Progress saves on its own.
  • Greater Ember CityDowntown, the Heights, Palm Grove, the Foundry, Port Ember, the beach and the airport.
  • Online with friendsHost your world and share a 6-letter join code. Chat, optional PvP, shared cars and aircraft.
  • RosyShe fights for you, plays fetch, learns tricks at the Bark Park and wears whatever collar you buy her.
  • Cars, helicopters, planesBuy a ride at Ember Motors or take one. Aircraft unlock as the story goes on.
  • Side jobs and turfProperties, gang turf wars, weather, and 24 hidden Ember Tokens to find.

OFFAL

HOLLOW PINES MEATWORKS · NIGHT SHIFT

You work the night shift at a slaughterhouse that goes down further than the plans say. Every dead worker swallowed a brass ID tag, and the freight lift only goes deeper when you feed it tags. Cut them out. It is slow, and it is loud. The Foreman hunts by sound and by light, and one hit from his hook kills you.

CONTENT WARNINGGraphic gore, mutilated bodies, sudden loud noises and flashing images. Play with headphones and the lights off. Needs a keyboard and mouse.

ON THE FLOOR

  • Puzzles on every floorDead power, sealed shutters with codes in blood, a jammed counterweight, steam valves, and freezer drawers in the morgue.
  • The ForemanCrouch in the dark and he can't see you. Shine your torch in his face and he will. From B5 there are two of him.
  • LockersHide, and hold your breath when he comes close. If he watched you climb in, he will open it.
  • OvertimeThe endless mode. Random floors, random puzzles, and your deepest floor goes on your time card.
  • Saves the exact momentWhere you stand, what you carry, every half-cut body. Continue puts you right back.
  • All of it made in codeTextures, models, gore and every sound are generated, with positional HRTF audio.
