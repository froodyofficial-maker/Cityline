NEW HIRE ORIENTATION

  1. Every worker on the line swallowed their brass ID tag. The freight lift only goes down when you feed it tags.
  2. Find the bodies. Hold Q to listen for the flies: a red arrow shows the way to the nearest body, and back to the lift once you have every tag. Hold E to cut the tag out. It is slow and it is loud.
  3. Bring the tags back to the lift, feed the panel, pull the lever, and go deeper. There is no bottom.
  4. The Foreman is still on shift. He hunts by sound and by light. Don't run unless you have to. Never shine your torch in his face.
  5. One hit from his hook kills you. Syringes (R) give you a few seconds of running that never runs out.
  6. Most floors have a job besides the tags: no power, a sealed shutter, a jammed counterweight, steam on the brake. The objective line at the top always says what’s next.
  7. Hide in lockers with E. If he comes close, hold SPACE to hold your breath. If he watched you climb in, it will not help.
  8. If you hear the hook dragging, it's patrolling. If it goes quiet, it's running.
