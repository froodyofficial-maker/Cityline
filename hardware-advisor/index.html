How it works

How GearGuide picks

Every product gets one score out of 100, built from what you told us. Nothing here is sponsored, and no product is ranked higher for being popular.

1

Game fit

Each game asks different things of your gear: fast reactions, precise aim, lots of keybinds, hearing footsteps, sharp visuals. We blend the games you play, then see how well each product delivers. If your games barely use a product type (say, a mouse for a controller game), game fit counts for less and comfort and value decide instead.

2

Comfort

Your hand length, grip, handedness, weight preference, desk space and play habits decide whether something feels right. A mouse that is too big for your hand, or a monitor too large for your desk, loses points and tells you why.

3

Value & budget

Products inside your range come first, and cheaper ones inside it score a little higher. Over-budget options stay in the full list, and every card suggests a cheaper and a pricier alternative.

A varied shortlist

The top picks aren't six near-copies. You get the closest fit, plus a best-value pick, a lesser-known gem, a premium option and a different take, each labelled.

Lesser-known picks

Smaller brands are ranked on fit alone and tagged, so a great mouse nobody has heard of isn't buried.
