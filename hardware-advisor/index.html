How GearGuide picks

Every product gets one score out of 100, built from three things about you. Nothing here is sponsored, and no product is ranked higher for being popular.

1

Game fit

Each game asks different things of your gear: fast reactions, precise aim, lots of keybinds, hearing footsteps, sharp visuals. We blend the games you play, so one demanding game isn't drowned out by casual ones, then see how well each product delivers.

2

Comfort

Your hand length, grip, handedness, weight preference, desk space and play habits decide whether something feels right. A mouse that is too big for your hand, or a monitor too large for your desk, loses points and tells you why.

3

Budget

Products inside your range are shown first. Over-budget options stay in the full list, and every card suggests a cheaper and a pricier alternative so you can see what the money buys.

Lesser-known picks

Smaller brands are ranked on fit alone and tagged, so a great mouse nobody has heard of isn't buried.

Prices

Typical US prices, checked October 2026, shown next to the official list price and converted to your currency. Always check a live price before you buy.

No single best

A lower match can still be the right call for you. The percentage only reflects what you told us.
